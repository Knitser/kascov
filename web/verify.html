Or sign it by hand

Any wallet with a Sign Message feature works. Kastle: account menu, Sign Message. KasWare: settings, Sign Message. kaspa CLI: message sign <your-address>. Then paste the signature into /confirm back in Discord.

Signing a message is free and moves no funds. It cannot authorise a transaction. kascov will never ask for your seed phrase or private key, and this page never sees either. If anything asks you for one, it is not us.
