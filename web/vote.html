the audit vote

Verified holders vote on what kascov audits next. This page is the public tally: counts only, never who voted for what.

The vote accelerates the audit queue. It never decides a verdict. A verdict comes from the chain and only from the chain, and kascov may audit anything at any time, voted for or not. Holding a token buys no influence over what kascov.io reports.

The tally lives at /vote-tally.json and this box renders it in your browser. With JavaScript off, read the file directly; it is the same data, counts and rules included.

The rules

  • One verified holder, one vote per round. Voting again before the round closes replaces the earlier ballot, it never adds a second one.
  • Voting needs a proven balance of at least 100 $KASCOV. The dust floor is policy, not physics, and changes only by a commit to the public repo.
  • Rounds close when the operator closes them, or after five days, whichever comes first. Closing freezes the counts and deletes the ballots themselves.
  • The vote accelerates the audit queue; it never decides a verdict.
  • kascov may audit anything at any time, voted for or not.

The same rules ship inside the tally file itself, so the counts cannot be quoted without their own limits attached.

How to vote

In the kascov Discord: verify an address with /verify, then cast with /vote choice:<number>. The bot’s full terms and privacy are at kascov.io/bot, including exactly what is stored while a round is open.
